About Me

I’m Alex, a Breton living in London. I’ve started capturing the world around me and sharing my perspective through photography. Taking good pictures is a journey that requires years of practice, and I am just beginning this adventure. Originally from Brittany, France, I moved to London over 10 years ago. Here, I found the love of my life and raised an exceptional son, and now I’ve grown roots that keep me here. Join me on this visual journey as I explore light, mood, and moments in everyday life.
